# Portfolio

Read `DESIGN.md` before changing anything here and follow it. It is the rulebook for colour, type, pictures, the swarm build attributes, motion and copy.

- Stack: Astro (static), Geist, GSAP. Content data in `src/data/site.ts`, posts in `src/content/blog`.
- Build engine: `src/scripts/swarm.ts`. Motion: `src/scripts/motion.ts`.
- Dev server: `npx astro dev --port 4331` (it runs in the background; `npx astro dev stop` to stop it).
